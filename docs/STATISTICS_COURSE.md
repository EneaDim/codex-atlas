# Statistics Codex — course coverage

The Statistics Codex is based only on the six notebooks supplied for the course.

## Covered chapters

1. Events and probability
2. Probability on finite spaces, combinatorics and sampling
3. Conditional probability, Bayes and independence
4. Multiple independence and random variables
5. Distributions, CDF, density and expected value
6. Variance, standard deviation and Tchebycheff inequalities

The map intentionally follows the terminology, formulas, examples and progression of those notebooks. Topics not present in the supplied material are not added silently.

## Concept-card structure

Each leaf concept may contain:

- a short explanation;
- one or more formulas;
- explanations of formula terms;
- a worked example;
- a source chapter marker.

This pack does not rely on Wikipedia for its primary explanation.
